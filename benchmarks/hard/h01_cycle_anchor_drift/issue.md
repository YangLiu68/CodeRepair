# Customers who signed up at the end of a month get billed earlier every month

Support escalation, three customers so far. All of them started their subscription on
January 31.

Their invoices were dated February 28 (expected, February is short), then March 28, April 28
and May 28. Our terms say the billing day is the day you signed up, "or the last day of the
month when that day does not exist". Customers who signed up on the 10th or the 15th are not
affected.

A customer on a quarterly plan who signed up on November 30 reports the same thing: their
invoice after the February one came on May 29 instead of May 30.

Renewals should go back to the 31st (or the 30th) after a short month. Finance will correct
the invoices that were already sent.
